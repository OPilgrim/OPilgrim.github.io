I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the [Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health](https://sph.nus.edu.sg/), [National University of Singapore](https://nus.edu.sg/), advised by Assoc. Prof. Mengling 'Mornin' Feng. My current work focuses on AI for public health, in particular self-improving AI agents that can evaluate their own progress and keep learning when reliable feedback is scarce or ill-defined, with applications to complex healthcare tasks such as mental health counseling.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was an Intermediate Engineer at NLPR, [Institute of Automation](https://ia.cas.cn/), [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://english.cas.cn/), where I was very fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Qiang Liu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=D-lKLcMAAAAJ), [Prof. Shu Wu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qVge6YYAAAAJ&hl=en), and [Prof. Liang Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8kzzUboAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I was advised by [Prof. Yuanping Chen](https://cnic.cas.cn/sourcedb_cnic_cas/zw/zjrc/dsdw/202107/t20210702_6125751.html) and [Prof. Xiaoqian Zhu](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=1zMMq0QAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) from [Computer Network Information Center](https://cnic.cas.cn/), University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I obtained my master's degree.

My earlier research focused on deep learning, natural language processing, and agent augmentation, particularly in event extraction, fact-checking, graph learning, tool and retrieval-augmented generation. I later extended my interest to multi-modal foundation models in AI4Science.

You can find my CV here: [CV](../assets/YunyueSu.pdf), and my papers on the [Publications](/publications/) page. Please feel free to contact me via [Email](mailto:yunyue.su@u.nus.edu) or [WeChat](../images/wechat.png).
