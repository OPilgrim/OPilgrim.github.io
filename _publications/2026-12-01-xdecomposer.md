Real-world synthesis often produces complex mixtures whose constituent phases cannot be reliably disentangled from powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD). XDecomposer infers an unordered set of phase-resolved components, their mixture proportions and structural representations within a unified architecture, using phase-query-driven decomposition together with diffraction-consistent physical reconstruction. It substantially improves reconstruction accuracy and phase identification on simulated and experimental data.
