Mapping each tool to a unique new token causes the vocabulary to explode and leaves every tool semantically isolated. ToolWeaver instead encodes tools into hierarchical sequences generated by a tokenization process that weaves together a tool's intrinsic semantics with its co-usage patterns, and aligns the LLM to generate these codes. Evaluated with nearly 47,000 tools, it significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods.
