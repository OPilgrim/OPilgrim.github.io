Text-to-image models often misinterpret complex and lengthy prompts, resulting in semantic inconsistencies and missing details. GenPilot is a flexible, model-agnostic test-time prompt optimization strategy that operates directly on the input text, integrating error analysis, clustering-based adaptive exploration and fine-grained verification.
